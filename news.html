OCTOBER 2026 PUBLICATION

New review on near-infrared fluorescence lifetime imaging

Our recent collaborative review, “Near-infrared fluorescence lifetime imaging: fluorophores, methods, and applications,” was published in the Journal of Biomedical Optics. The article brings together information on near-infrared fluorophores, imaging systems, lifetime analysis, and biomedical applications.

2026 RESEARCH

Building a biophotonics research program at WCU

Our research directions at West Chester University include computational diffuse optical imaging, fluorescence spectroscopy, and uncertainty-aware reconstruction. Undergraduate research is an important part of the program, connecting classroom learning to practical problems in biomedical optics.
