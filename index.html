01 / OUR MISSION

Better images begin with a better understanding of light.

Biological tissue scatters light, making it difficult to recover what is happening beneath the surface. At PRISM Lab, we combine light-transport modeling, optical measurements, and image reconstruction to address that challenge.

Our work spans diffuse optical tomography, fluorescence lifetime imaging, and AI-assisted analysis, with a strong focus on undergraduate research.
