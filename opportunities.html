Opportunities

Learn, build, and discover.

PRISM Lab provides opportunities for students to work on research problems at the intersection of biomedical engineering, optics, and computing.

UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH

Interested in joining a project?

Students may gain experience in photon transport simulation, Python or MATLAB coding, optical measurements, and scientific communication. Projects are scoped according to a student's background and the needs of the research program.

Students with interests in biomedical engineering, optics, programming, or numerical modeling are encouraged to get in touch. Funding and openings change over time, so please email to ask about current availability.

STUDENT RESEARCH

What helps you get started

  • Curiosity about how light interacts with tissue
  • Willingness to learn coding or laboratory methods
  • Care with measurements, documentation, and analysis
  • Interest in presenting what you discover

No prior experience in all these areas is expected.

COLLABORATION

Research partnerships

We welcome collaborations in biomedical optics, inverse problems, fluorescence imaging, and applied AI. Please reach out with a short description of the idea or problem you have in mind.
