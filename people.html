LAB DIRECTOR

Navid Ibtehaj Nizam, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor · Biomedical Engineering
West Chester University

Dr. Nizam leads the PRISM Lab at West Chester University. His work focuses on computational biophotonics, biomedical optical imaging, fluorescence lifetime imaging, and deep learning-based reconstruction.

He received his Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in 2025. His research combines optical models, numerical methods, and experiments, with a particular interest in making quantitative imaging more accurate and useful.
